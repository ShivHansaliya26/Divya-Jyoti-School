Welcome to Divya Jyoti Public School

Divya Jyoti Public School is a premier English medium institution located in Kanore, District Udaipur, Rajasthan. We are dedicated to nurturing young minds with quality education, strong moral values, and holistic development.

Our school offers education from Play Group through Class 8th, with a special focus on making quality education accessible to children from all backgrounds — especially those from underprivileged families.

With a team of qualified and experienced teachers, daily English spoken classes, disciplined prayer sessions, and regular parent engagement, we ensure every child receives the attention they deserve.
